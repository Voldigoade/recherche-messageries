# Threat model

## Short definition

Specify what you protect, from whom, and under which constraints.


## Concrete example

Alice wants to hide her number from Bob; another person refuses a number even at the service. Their choices differ.


## What it protects

Makes comparisons and configuration conditions useful.


## What it does not protect

It is neither a guarantee nor a global score replacing unknowns.


## Why it matters

Two users can have different good choices without contradiction.
