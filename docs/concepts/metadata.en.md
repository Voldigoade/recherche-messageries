# Metadata

## Short definition

Data around the exchange: accounts, IP, timing, volume, devices or groups.


## Concrete example

Text is encrypted, but the relay sees a connection at 6 pm and its duration.


## What it protects

Minimising this data can reduce identification and reconstruction of relationships.


## What it does not protect

Encrypting the message body does not automatically erase transport or account data.


## Why it matters

These clues can link people even without reading their text.

<details class="technical-details"><summary>Technical details</summary><p>The observer matters: provider, peer, ISP, push and authorities have different visibility.</p></details>
