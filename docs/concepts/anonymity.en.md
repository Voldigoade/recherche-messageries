# Anonymity

## Short definition

A defined adversary cannot reasonably distinguish you within the relevant set of users.


## Concrete example

A phone-free account can still be identifiable by IP or an identity-linked payment.


## What it protects

Difficulty of attribution against the adversary and scenario actually studied.


## What it does not protect

Not an absolute app property; not a remedy for seizure or spyware.


## Why it matters

Asking “anonymous from whom?” avoids a false promise.
