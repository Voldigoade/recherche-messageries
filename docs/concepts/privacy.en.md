# Privacy and data minimisation

## Short definition

Reduce information about you that can be observed, retained or linked.


## Concrete example

Your message stays secret, but the service knows your number and last connection.


## What it protects

Reduces content or contextual data depending on architecture and settings.


## What it does not protect

Does not automatically mean anonymity or absence of all metadata.


## Why it matters

Information useful to an investigation is not limited to messages.

<details class="technical-details"><summary>Technical details</summary><p>Distinguish necessary access, claimed retention, observed retention and actually documented disclosure.</p></details>
