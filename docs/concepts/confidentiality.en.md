# Confidentiality

## Short definition

Limit who can access information, such as a conversation’s text.


## Concrete example

The service cannot read your message, but the intended recipient can.


## What it protects

Access by unauthorised people or systems, depending on the chosen mechanism.


## What it does not protect

By itself, does not establish whether identity, presence or timing is hidden.


## Why it matters

Do not confuse secrecy of text with secrecy of activity.
