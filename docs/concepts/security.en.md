# Security

## Short definition

Prevent or limit reading, tampering, impersonation and unavailability against a defined adversary.


## Concrete example

Bob must receive the correct message from the correct contact and be able to use the application.


## What it protects

Confidentiality, integrity, availability and authenticity, depending on the threat model.


## What it does not protect

The adjective “secure” does not guarantee all properties or versions.


## Why it matters

Choosing requires specifying what needs protection and from whom.

<details class="technical-details"><summary>Technical details</summary><p>A scope-limited audit does not establish the safety of the whole app, backend and current binaries.</p></details>
