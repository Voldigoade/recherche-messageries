# Pseudonymity

## Short definition

Use an identity different from your civil identity. It can still be linked to you.


## Concrete example

Alice uses a new alias but reuses her public photo: Bob can make the connection.


## What it protects

Identity separation from a specific observer, with suitable identifiers and behaviour.


## What it does not protect

Does not make registration number, IP, purchases, voice or habits invisible.


## Why it matters

Hiding a number from the contact is not hiding it from the provider.


## Example messenger behaviour

Signal can hide the number from the peer after configuration; the service still requires it.

<details class="technical-details"><summary>Technical details</summary><p>A stable pseudonym is linkable. Define the observer and linkage channels separately.</p></details>
