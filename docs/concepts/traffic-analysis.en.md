# Traffic analysis

## Short definition

Infer information from timing, sizes, volumes and paths without reading text.


## Concrete example

Two recurring connections appear seconds apart: an observer looks for correlation.


## What it protects

Padding, delays or cover traffic can limit some inferences depending on their design.


## What it does not protect

Encryption alone does not hide all these clues.


## Why it matters

Low-latency onion architecture does not prove global immunity.
