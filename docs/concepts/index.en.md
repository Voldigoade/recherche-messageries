# Learn the concepts

<p>Each concept starts with a simple answer, then an example, its limits and sources.</p><dl class="concept-index"><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/transit/">Encryption in transit</a></dt><dd>Protects the connection between two points. The server may be one of those points.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/e2ee/">End-to-end encryption — E2EE</a></dt><dd>Participants’ devices control content keys. The transport server should not read ordinary messages.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/confidentiality/">Confidentiality</a></dt><dd>Limit who can access information, such as a conversation’s text.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/security/">Security</a></dt><dd>Prevent or limit reading, tampering, impersonation and unavailability against a defined adversary.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/privacy/">Privacy and data minimisation</a></dt><dd>Reduce information about you that can be observed, retained or linked.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/metadata/">Metadata</a></dt><dd>Data around the exchange: accounts, IP, timing, volume, devices or groups.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/telemetry/">Telemetry</a></dt><dd>Data an application sends about its operation, such as errors or usage.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/pseudonymity/">Pseudonymity</a></dt><dd>Use an identity different from your civil identity. It can still be linked to you.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/anonymity/">Anonymity</a></dt><dd>A defined adversary cannot reasonably distinguish you within the relevant set of users.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/network-anonymity/">Network anonymity</a></dt><dd>Reduce the ability to link traffic to your network address or its destination.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/unlinkability/">Unlinkability</a></dt><dd>Make it difficult to link two identities, relationships or activities.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/traffic-analysis/">Traffic analysis</a></dt><dd>Infer information from timing, sizes, volumes and paths without reading text.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/forward-secrecy/">Forward secrecy — PFS</a></dt><dd>Keys compromised later should not reveal old messages protected by deleted ephemeral keys.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/post-compromise/">Post-compromise security</a></dt><dd>A session can regain confidentiality after temporary state theft and safe key evolution.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/verification/">Key verification</a></dt><dd>Check that the key used matches the key expected for your contact.</dd><dt><a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/threat-model/">Threat model</a></dt><dd>Specify what you protect, from whom, and under which constraints.</dd></dl>
