# Unlinkability

## Short definition

Make it difficult to link two identities, relationships or activities.


## Concrete example

Alice uses separate invitations but copies the same alias and avatar: separation fails.


## What it protects

Reduces some joins between accounts or contacts.


## What it does not protect

Not voluntarily shared clues, Discord timing or device traces.


## Why it matters

A stable identifier can link contexts even without a civil name.


## Example messenger behaviour

SimpleX requires no global ID; the initial invitation from Discord remains a trace.

<details class="technical-details"><summary>Technical details</summary><p>Separate account identifiers, relationship identifiers and network observations.</p></details>
