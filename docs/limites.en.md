# Limitations

<p>Medium for well-documented properties; lower for production metadata and untested versions.</p><ul><li>Compromised / unlocked device and spyware.</li><li>Copying, screenshots or disclosure by the contact.</li><li>Initial Discord traces, reused alias, avatar, voice or files.</li><li>Global correlation; relay, ISP, telecom, store and backup data depending on the path.</li></ul><p>High-impact unknowns include Session fixes and independent retests, exact mobile push payloads, relay logging, Threema desktop PFS and retests, current distributed binaries, and forensic results pinned to device, OS, app version and locked state. A historical extraction cannot be assumed to work after a documented fix.</p><a class="fact-source" href="https://github.com/Voldigoade/recherche-messageries/blob/main/research/rapport-final-messageries.md#L397" aria-label="Evidence:  — report.limits">[1]</a><p>The detailed evidence is maintained in the French <a href="/recherche-messageries/limites/">Phase 2 register</a>. The translation is a restatement, not new evidence.</p>
