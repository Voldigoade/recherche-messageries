## Understand before choosing

<div class="context-help"><details><summary>End-to-end encryption — E2EE</summary><p>Participants’ devices control content keys. The transport server should not read ordinary messages. <a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/e2ee/">Learn more</a></p></details><details><summary>Metadata</summary><p>Data around the exchange: accounts, IP, timing, volume, devices or groups. <a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/metadata/">Learn more</a></p></details><details><summary>Pseudonymity</summary><p>Use an identity different from your civil identity. It can still be linked to you. <a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/pseudonymity/">Learn more</a></p></details><details><summary>Network anonymity</summary><p>Reduce the ability to link traffic to your network address or its destination. <a href="/recherche-messageries/en/concepts/network-anonymity/">Learn more</a></p></details></div>
